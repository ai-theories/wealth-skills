# Regulatory Compliance & Audit Guidelines

All AI skills and MCP connectors within the **`wealth-skills`** library operate under strict compliance standards tailored to US Financial Services, SEC regulation, and FINRA rules.

---

## 1. Regulatory Context

When AI agents process data or generate content for US Wealth Management, they touch several regulatory frameworks:

- **SEC Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (Rule 206(4)-1 - Marketing Rule)**: Rules governing client communications, performance presentations, and disclosures.
- **FINRA Rule 2210 (Communications with the Public)**: Standards for retail communications, institutional communications, and correspondence.
- **FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision)**: Requirement for supervisory review of financial advice, client briefs, and account operations.
- **SEC Regulation S-P (Privacy of Consumer Financial Information)**: Safeguarding Non-Public Personal Information (NPI).

---

## 2. Mandatory Audit Trail Architecture

Every `wealth-skills` prompt output MUST include a structured JSON or YAML audit trail header at the bottom of the response:

```yaml
---
audit_metadata:
  skill_pack: "wealth-portfolio"
  capability_id: "T025"
  methodology: "Factor Risk Attribution & Mean-Variance Scenario"
  data_sources:
    - "SEC EDGAR 10-K (CIK: 0000320193)"
    - "FRED Interest Rate Series (GS10)"
  confidence_score: 0.96
  disclaimer_appended: true
  requires_human_approval: true
---
```

---

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Enforcement Rules

AI agents using `wealth-skills` are **strictly prohibited** from performing autonomous execution without human review for any action flagged with `requires_human_approval: true`.

| Action Category | HITL Required? | Approval Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| **Trade Order Execution** | **YES (Mandatory)** | Licensed Advisor (Series 7 / 65 / 66) must review & confirm payload. |
| **Money Movement / ACAT Transfer** | **YES (Mandatory)** | Dual approval: Client signature + Compliance review. |
| **Legal / Estate Doc Generation** | **YES (Mandatory)** | Estate Attorney or CFP review required prior to client signing. |
| **Client Portfolio Briefing** | Optional | Advisor review recommended before sending to retail client. |
| **Market Research / 10-K Summary** | No | Informational output; no direct financial impact. |
